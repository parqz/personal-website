I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Georgia State University, advised by
Dr. Rajshekhar Sunderraman and Dr. Yi Ding (University of Tennessee, Knoxville), and a graduate
research assistant at GSU's CHAI Center (previously the INSPIRE Center and the TCV Initiative). I work at the intersection of **efficient and trustworthy
AI**: building AI systems that are not only capable, but also computationally efficient, reliable,
privacy-preserving and practical to deploy.

A common theme runs through my work: improving AI under real-world constraints, whether limited
compute, noisy supervision, privacy requirements or undesirable model behavior. I'm most drawn to
research that connects algorithmic ideas with systems-level considerations, so that AI becomes
more efficient, trustworthy and deployable in practice. My work spans three
[research areas](#research): AI efficiency and optimization; robust, equitable and trustworthy AI
(two EMNLP 2026 papers); and privacy-preserving machine learning with homomorphic encryption
(IEEE TPS, ACNS and IEEE S&P).

I welcome collaborations on research and applied research in machine learning, large language
models, efficient AI systems, privacy and trustworthy AI.
